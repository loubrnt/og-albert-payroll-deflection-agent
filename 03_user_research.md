## Interview guide — adapted to our case (keep it about the past)
For a **support agent**:
1. Tell me about the last "how do I…" ticket you answered. What happened, step by step?
2. How did you find the right article? How long did that take? What if it was out of date?
3. What did you do the last time you were not sure you were allowed to answer?
4. Tell me about the last month end (or the busiest day you remember). What did your queue look like?
5. When an answer went wrong, what happened next? Who told you?
6. If you could change one thing in that moment, what would it be?

For an **HR / payroll user**:
1. Tell me about the last time you could not find how to do something in your payroll tool. What were you trying to do?
2. What did you try first? Where did you look? How long before you asked someone?
3. When you contacted support, what did you write, and how long did you wait?
4. What did you do while waiting? What did it cost you?
5. Was there a time an answer from support was wrong? What happened?

*Avoid:* "Would you use an AI that…?" · "Do you like this idea?" · "How much would you pay?"
