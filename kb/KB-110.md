# KB-110 — Password and two-factor authentication

*Help center (synthetic article for the course). Last reviewed: 2026-10-20.*

## Reset your password
On the sign-in page click Forgot password, enter your email address and follow the link you receive. The link is valid for one hour.

## Set up two-factor authentication
Open your profile, click Security, then Enable two-factor authentication. Scan the code with an authenticator app and enter the six digits to confirm. Keep the recovery codes in a safe place.
