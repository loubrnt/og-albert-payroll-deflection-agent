# KB-104 — Record an absence or paid leave

*Help center (synthetic article for the course). Last reviewed: 2026-10-20.*

## Add an absence
Open the employee record, click Absences, then Add. Pick the type (paid leave, sick leave, unpaid leave), the first and last day, and save.

## Approve a leave request
Requests made by employees appear under Requests. Click a request to approve or decline it. Approved leave is added to the calendar and to the next payroll run.
