# KB-106 — Invite an accountant and manage access

*Help center (synthetic article for the course). Last reviewed: 2026-10-20.*

## Invite a user
Go to Settings, then Users, then Invite. Enter the email address and choose a role: Admin, Payroll manager or Read only. The person receives an invitation link.

## Roles and what they can do
Admin can change company settings. Payroll manager can run payroll. Read only can view and download reports. Change a role from the Users list at any time.
