# KB-112 — Archive a departed employee

*Help center (synthetic article for the course). Last reviewed: 2026-10-20.*

## Archive the record
Open the employee record, click Actions, then Archive employee, and enter the last working day. The employee is removed from future payroll runs but their history stays available.

## Restore an archived employee
Open People, filter on Archived, open the record and click Restore. The employee is added again to the next payroll run.
