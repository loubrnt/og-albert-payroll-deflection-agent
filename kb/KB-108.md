# KB-108 — Connect the time-tracking integration

*Help center (synthetic article for the course). Last reviewed: 2026-10-20.*

## Connect your tool
Go to Settings, then Integrations, choose your time-tracking tool and click Connect. Sign in with your account on the tool and accept the connection.

## Choose what is synchronised
After connecting, choose which teams are synchronised and whether worked hours or only absences are sent to the product. The first synchronisation takes a few minutes.
