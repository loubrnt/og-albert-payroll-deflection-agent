# KB-103 — Download and send payslips

*Help center (synthetic article for the course). Last reviewed: 2026-10-20.*

## Download payslips in a batch
Open Payroll, choose the closed pay period, click Payslips, then Download all. A zip file with one PDF per employee is prepared.

## Send payslips by email
In the same screen click Send to employees. Each employee receives a secure link to their payslip. You can resend to a single employee from their record.
