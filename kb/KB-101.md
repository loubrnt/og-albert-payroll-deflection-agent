# KB-101 — Export the payroll journal

*Help center (synthetic article for the course). Last reviewed: 2026-10-20.*

## Where to find the export
Open Reports, then Payroll, then Export. Since release 5.2 the export is no longer under Settings. Choose Payroll journal and click Export.

## Choose the period and the format
Select the pay period in the top bar, then pick CSV or Excel. The file downloads in a few seconds and includes one line per employee.

## Release 5.2: what moved
In release 5.2 the payroll CSV export moved from Settings to Reports, then Payroll, then Export. Saved export templates were kept and appear in the Template drop-down.
