# KB-102 — Add a new employee

*Help center (synthetic article for the course). Last reviewed: 2026-10-20.*

## Create the employee record
Go to People, click Add employee, then enter the first name, last name, start date and job title. Save to create the record.

## Attach the contract
In the employee record open Contract, choose the contract type and the working hours, then upload the signed document if you have one. The employee appears in the next payroll run.
