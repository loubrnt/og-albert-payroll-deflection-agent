# KB-107 — Import employees from a spreadsheet

*Help center (synthetic article for the course). Last reviewed: 2026-10-20.*

## Download the template
Open People, click Import, then Download template. Fill one line per employee and keep the column names unchanged.

## Upload the file
Click Upload file and select your CSV. A preview shows the lines that will be created and the lines with a problem. Fix the problems and upload again, then confirm.
