## Session 2 — the cost of error
- Which cost-of-error line is a **legal or reputational** risk, not just money? **Legal & payroll-rule deadlines** (a wrong DSN/URSSAF or leave-rule deadline can make the customer file late and pay a penalty — and the error depends on jurisdiction/collective agreement), and **pay figures** (a wrong amount quoted to an HR manager can end in a labour-law dispute and a public loss of trust). That is why both are out of scope in the base case (`02_sizing.xlsx`, Include = 0); legal deadlines enter only with a jurisdiction check, in the optimistic scenario.
- Who in the company **owns** that risk? The **Head of Legal & Compliance** owns the legal-deadline content and its validation; the **Head of Support** owns the agent and the escalation rule; the DPO owns the data line.

| 4 | **Rights** | Synthetic data: no personal data. In production: the DPA covers support processing; ticket retention 12 months (proposal); the sub-processor list is updated for the LLM provider (EU hosting, no training on our data). | guess | DPO |
| 5 | **Leakage** | Eval-set files are separate from the router's training file and the help center; the eval-set is never used to tune the prompt or the threshold (any ticket we looked at while building becomes demo data and is named in `evals/report_v0.md`). In production, retrieval is limited to the shared help center, so one customer's ticket can never appear in another's answer. | measured | team |
| 6 | **Freshness** | Each article carries a "last reviewed" date; an article older than 6 months, or about a law that changed in January, triggers escalation instead of an answer. Release notes are versioned. | judgement | Content team |

- **Purpose limitation (GDPR art. 5):** data is used to answer support questions and to evaluate the agent, nothing else; tickets and logs are kept 12 months, then deleted.
- **Isolation between customers:** v1 retrieves only from the shared help center; no account data is injected into the prompt. When account context is added (category 3, later), retrieval is filtered by customer ID before the model sees anything.
- **Known gaps by segment:** non-French/English tickets; customers on legacy plans; questions about the last release (help-center lag); month-end and year-end periods (drift).

### Trust level per step
| Step | Level | Why (cost of an error) |
|---|---|---|
| Read the ticket and classify | L4 above a confidence threshold, otherwise escalate | A wrong route is cheap if the policy check follows, expensive if a legal or pay ticket is treated as how-to (€300–500). |
| Retrieve passages | L3 | An irrelevant passage leads to a wrong answer, caught by citations and sampling. |
| Draft the answer from the passages | L3 on "how do I…" (5 % sampled), person only on everything else | A wrong how-to costs a reopened ticket (~€8). |
| Policy check (no amount, deadline or refund) | L4 — a rule, it can only block | A missed block costs €300–500 and a contract risk. |
| Send the answer | L3 on "how do I…", person only otherwise | Same. |
| Handle escalations | Person only | Money, law and emotion. |
